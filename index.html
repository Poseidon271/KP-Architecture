Studio Profile

“As the third generation of architects, we expertly blend tradition with innovation.”

K.P. Architects is a dynamic architectural and engineering practice founded in 2012 by Ar. Praneet Khare, Ar. Shikha Ujjwal, and Er. Pragalbha Khare in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Excelling in Architecture, Interior Design, and Structural Engineering, our portfolio encompasses over 1,000 projects across commercial, residential, hospitality, and industrial developments.

By viewing design through the lens of Generation Z, we harmonize timeless values with modern trends, crafting a future that honors the past while embracing the present. This balance between novelty and tradition allows us to create spaces that are both inspiring and enduring, upholding the core principles of architectural integrity.

Generational Heritage & Lineage

The firm's foundational roots trace back to the KBD master lineage originating in 1919. In 2012, this century-spanning legacy evolved into the contemporary multidisciplinary practice of K.P. Architects, unifying heritage craftsmanship with computational structural detailing and turnkey delivery.
